🌱 What is Hydroponics?

Hydroponics is a method of growing plants without soil. The roots receive water containing the nutrients needed for healthy plant growth.

🎯 Aim of the Project

To demonstrate a soil-less farming system that can save water, use limited space efficiently and support sustainable agriculture using smart automation and solar energy.

⚙️ How Our Model Works

  1. The solar panel supplies electrical power to the system.
  2. A timer controls when the water pump operates.
  3. The pump sends nutrient-rich water through the pipe network.
  4. The water reaches the plant roots through the growing channels.
  5. The water collects in the reservoir and can be circulated again.
  6. Plants grow without traditional soil.

✨ Main Features

🌿 Soil-less Farming Plants grow using a nutrient solution instead of soil.
💧 Water Saving The circulating system can reduce water wastage compared with conventional farming.
☀️ Solar Powered Solar energy can provide power for the pump and automation system.
⏱️ Smart Automation A timer can operate the pump at programmed intervals.
🏙️ Small-Space Farming Hydroponics can be useful where traditional farmland is limited.
♻️ Sustainable Agriculture Efficient water use and controlled growing can support sustainable farming.

🔧 Components Used

  • Solar panel
  • Water pump
  • Timer switch
  • Water reservoir
  • PVC pipes and outlets
  • Growing/support sheet
  • Plants and nutrient solution
  • Electrical wires and connections

🌍 Advantages

  • Does not require traditional soil.
  • Can use water efficiently through circulation.
  • Can be operated in comparatively small spaces.
  • Allows better control over the plant root environment.
  • Can be combined with solar energy and automation.
Important: Actual water savings and growth speed depend on the hydroponic design, crop, climate and operating conditions.

🚀 Future Improvements

The model can be upgraded with an ESP32/Arduino, water-level monitoring, pH monitoring, nutrient (TDS/EC) monitoring, temperature/humidity sensing, automatic alerts and a live online dashboard.
